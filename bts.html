Behind the Scenes of the Oops!... I Did It Again Music Video by MTV Making the Video

Fun Facts About the Oops!... I Did It Again Music Video:
  • The iconic red suit outfit was made in less than 24 hours by Michael Bush.
  • Britney came up with the idea to dance on Mars.
  • The dance moves were choreographed by Tina Landon.
  • The scene where the astronaut gives Britney the "Heart of the Ocean" necklace was a direct nod to the 1997 film, Titanic.
  • Despite popular belief, the red suit outfit did not have a headset mic.
